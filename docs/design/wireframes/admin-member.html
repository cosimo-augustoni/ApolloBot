Annotations

  • Same view as /me (same component), for any member; only admins can open it.
  • Header adds avatar, name, tracking status and platform. An untracked member without data shows the empty state wording in third person ("Für Anna werden keine Daten erfasst").
  • Below the stats: links to the member's recaps (same recap pages as the member sees).
  • Date range presets: 7 Tage, 30 Tage (default), Dieser Monat, Dieses Jahr, Benutzerdefiniert (date picker). Changing the range reloads all cards; no auto refresh.
  • All numbers come from the date-range stats layer (scope §5.3). Deltas compare with the previous range of the same length; when the member was not tracked for the whole previous range the delta shows –.
  • "Erfasst an X von Y Tagen": untracked days (tracking_periods) are left out of averages.
  • Each card shows the style guide EmptyState ("Noch keine Daten") when its range has no data.
  • Spiele is a bonus section: hidden completely when there is no presence data (Discord only, incomplete by design).
  • Bot messages are excluded. Threads roll up to their parent channel.
  • At 360px tiles stack (one per row), charts keep full card width; the heatmap keeps 7 × 24 cells (≈ 12px each).
