Annotations

  • Same view as /me (same magazine component, scope D16), so admins see exactly what the member sees; it sits inside the admin shell with the drawer.
  • Header adds avatar, name, tracking status and platform, plus the period switch (the member header is not shown here).
  • Covers and the period switch open the member's recaps at /admin/members/{id}/recaps/{period}, again in the magazine layout.
  • An untracked member without data shows the empty state wording in third person ("Für Anna werden keine Daten erfasst").
  • Magazine layout (scope D16): one long editorial page in chapters (Kanäle, Voice, Zeiten, Spiele). Each chapter opens with a big headline and one key number, then its chart or list.
  • The row of recap covers at the top replaces a separate recaps list (there is no /me/recaps list page): running month (dashed), past months, years (darker). Newest first; the row scrolls sideways inside itself on small screens, the page never does.
  • Sticky period pill: shows the period (opens the same period switch as the header) and, from 760px up, jump links to the chapters. On phones only the period is shown.
  • Headline sentences are generated from the numbers ("Am liebsten bist du mittwochabends da"); keep templates simple and fall back to a plain title when data is thin.
  • The games chapter is a bonus and is left out completely without presence data. Chapters with no data in the period are left out too. Bot messages are excluded; threads roll up to their parent channel.
  • At 360px: the 4 numbers become a 2×2 grid, chapter headline and key number stack, two-column parts stack.
