Annotations

  • Recap detail = the same magazine layout for the snapshot period, plus "Erstellt am …" and prev/next. The selected cover is outlined.
  • Numbers come from the fixed snapshot (scope §5.3), so the page loads instantly. Hi-fi cover and card design is #45.
  • Concept B · Magazine: one long editorial page in chapters (Kanäle, Voice, Zeiten, Spiele). Each chapter opens with a big headline and one key number, then its chart or list.
  • The row of recap covers at the top replaces a recaps list: running month (dashed), past months, years (darker). The row scrolls sideways inside itself on small screens; the page never does.
  • Sticky period pill: shows the period (opens the same period switch as the header) and, from 760px up, jump links to the chapters. On phones only the period is shown.
  • Headline sentences are generated from the numbers ("Am liebsten bist du mittwochabends da"); keep templates simple and fall back to a plain title when data is thin.
  • The games chapter is a bonus and is left out completely without presence data. Chapters with no data in the period are left out too.
  • At 360px: the 4 numbers become a 2×2 grid, chapter headline and key number stack, two-column parts stack.
  • Member shell (all concepts): no drawer. Header = Apollo logo (home), period switch ("Oktober 2026 ▾": running month, past months/years, Benutzerdefiniert) and avatar menu (Privatsphäre, Datenschutz, Abmelden; "Admin" only for admins, leads to the existing admin shell).
  • Menus are <details> here so they work without JS; real pages use MudMenu.
  • Privatsphäre currently links to the existing me-privacy.html; it gets restyled to the chosen concept later.
